# Structure Review

Judge structures by frequency, function, and genre. No construction below is an authorship test.

## High-value review targets

### Repeated negative parallelism

Several variants of `not X, but Y`, `not only X`, or `X is not the problem; Y is` create a predictable reveal. State Y directly unless the contrast changes the claim.

### Repeated summaries

Generated drafts often preview a point, explain it, restate it at the end of the section, and repeat it in a conclusion. Keep the version with the strongest evidence.

### Empty taxonomies

Headings and numbered lists can make thin analysis appear complete. Keep a taxonomy only when the categories are distinct, exhaustive enough for the claim, and useful to the reader.

### Mechanical balance

A caveat after every claim can hide the author's conclusion. Preserve uncertainty in proportion to the evidence; do not manufacture two equal sides.

### Uniform cadence

Repeated sentence frames and equal paragraph lengths can make prose feel generated. Vary them when the argument calls for a different pace. Do not add random variation.

### False specificity

Names, figures, quotations, and citations can sound authoritative while being wrong. Verify them before polishing the sentence.

## Genre-aware choices

- Passive voice suits methods, incident reports, and cases where the actor is unknown or irrelevant.
- First person suits personal essays and can clarify responsibility in research or business writing.
- Direct address suits instructions but can sound intrusive in reports.
- Questions suit interviews and essays but can become empty setup devices.
- Fragments suit dialogue, advertising, and some personal prose; repeated fragments weaken analysis.
- Three-item lists and em dashes are ordinary punctuation and rhetoric. Review repetition, not existence.

## Revision test

After restructuring, check that every paragraph advances at least one of these: evidence, reasoning, scene, decision, instruction, or necessary context. A paragraph that only announces importance or summarizes adjacent text can usually go.
