# Changelog

## 2026-09-11

### Added

**Modes (SKILL.md)**
- Edit (default): rewrite, keep the writer's voice, change only what the rules require
- Detect/audit: name the pattern, quote the line, give a short fix, do not rewrite

**Voice (SKILL.md)**
- Preserve distinctive cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, and digressions
- Minimum effective edit; do not flatten every draft into the same punchy house style

**Phrases (references/phrases.md)**
- Faux-insight setups: "What nobody tells you", "The part everyone misses"
- Importance puffery: "marks a pivotal moment", "stands as a testament"
- Weasel attribution: "experts agree", "studies show" (name the source or cut; never invent one)
- Interpretive metadiscourse: "the key point is", "this distinction matters"

**Structures (references/structures.md)**
- Colon-reveal drama: "The best part: it learns."
- Superficial -ing analysis: "highlighting the team's commitment..."
- Synonym cycling for the same actor or tool
- Fake-profound kickers and summary-recap endings
- Formatting slop: emoji headings, decorative mid-sentence bold, tiny header spam

**Eval (references/eval.md)**
- Post-edit pass/fail checklist aligned with scoring

**Examples (references/examples.md)**
- Five new before/after pairs (faux-insight, puffery, weasel cites, synonym cycling, voice kept)

### Changed

- Adverbs: cut empty ones; keep adverbs that carry emphasis, uncertainty, or spoken rhythm
- Em dashes: still a rhythm crutch; rare use allowed in longer drafts when a comma, period, or parenthesis is worse
- Scoring Authenticity question now asks whether the draft sounds like this writer
- README "What it catches" updated for the new tells, soft rules, and voice

## 2026-01-13

### Added

**Phrases (references/phrases.md)**
- Throat-clearing: "Here's what I find interesting", "Here's the problem though"
- Performative emphasis: "creeps in", "I promise", "They exist, I promise"
- Telling instead of showing: "This is genuinely hard", "This is what leadership actually looks like"

**Structures (references/structures.md)**
- Binary contrasts: "Not X. But Y.", "It's not this. It's that.", "stops being X and starts being Y"
- Rhythm patterns: staccato fragmentation, dashes for dramatic pause, hedging as reassurance
- Word patterns: absolute words (always, never, everyone, etc.), AI-overused intensifiers (deeply, truly, fundamentally, inherently, simply, literally, inevitably)
